Understanding Solar Energy Systems

Solar energy systems convert sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic (PV) panels. They reduce reliance on the national grid, lower electricity bills, and provide clean, sustainable power. AegisVision provides professional design, installation, and maintenance to ensure maximum efficiency and long‑term reliability.

Types of Solar Power Systems

Choose the right configuration for your energy needs

Off‑Grid Solar System

100% independent · Battery storage · For remote areas

Off‑grid systems operate completely independently of the national grid. They include solar panels, charge controllers, battery banks, and inverters. Ideal for rural properties, farms, lodges, and areas with unreliable or no grid access. Energy is stored during the day for use at night or during cloudy periods.

Request Quote

On‑Grid (Grid‑Tied) Solar System

Connected to utility grid · No batteries · Net metering

On‑grid systems connect directly to the national electricity grid. They supply solar power to your building during the day and draw from the grid when solar is insufficient. Excess energy can be exported to the grid (where net metering is available). This is the most cost‑effective option for urban homes and offices.

Request Quote

Hybrid Solar System

Grid + batteries · Backup power · Energy independence

Hybrid systems combine the best of off‑grid and on‑grid. They include battery storage but remain connected to the grid. During a power outage, the batteries provide backup. You can store excess solar energy and use it during peak tariff hours, saving money while ensuring uninterrupted power.

Key Components of a Solar Power System

The building blocks of reliable renewable energy

Solar Panels (PV Modules)

Monocrystalline · Polycrystalline · Bifacial

Solar panels capture sunlight and convert it to DC electricity. Monocrystalline panels offer the highest efficiency, while polycrystalline are more affordable. Bifacial panels generate power from both sides. We supply Tier‑1 brands with 25‑year performance warranties.

Request Quote

Solar Inverters

String inverters · Microinverters · Hybrid inverters

Inverters convert DC power from solar panels into AC power for your building. String inverters are cost‑effective for standard installations. Microinverters optimise each panel individually. Hybrid inverters manage both solar and battery storage seamlessly.

Request Quote

Energy Storage (Batteries)

Lithium‑ion · Lead‑acid · Deep cycle

Batteries store excess solar energy for use at night or during grid outages. Lithium‑ion batteries are compact, long‑lasting, and require no maintenance. Lead‑acid batteries are more affordable for smaller systems. We help you choose the right capacity for your energy needs.

Request Quote

Charge Controllers

PWM · MPPT · Overcharge protection

Charge controllers regulate the voltage and current from solar panels to batteries, preventing overcharging and extending battery life. MPPT controllers are more efficient, especially in cloudy conditions or low light.
