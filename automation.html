What is Smart Building Automation?

Smart automation connects your building’s core systems – lighting, HVAC, security, and appliances – into a unified network that can be controlled remotely via smartphone, tablet, or voice. AegisVision delivers scalable, future‑proof automation that reduces energy waste, improves occupant comfort, and provides real‑time insights.
