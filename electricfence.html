How Electric Fencing Works

An electric fence energizer sends a high‑voltage, low‑current pulse along the fence wires at regular intervals (about one second). When an intruder touches the wires, they receive a short, safe but memorable shock — and the system triggers an alarm. AegisVision installations comply with international safety standards, ensuring protection without harm.
